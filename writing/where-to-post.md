# Where to post, in order

## 1. Hacker News — Show HN
Title (80 char limit):
    Show HN: I invented a CVE number to test my tool. It was real.

Alt, if you want the measurement to lead:
    Show HN: Substantiate – check if a bug report's claims resolve against your repo

Post the URL to the repo. Then immediately add a comment with the post body.
Best window: Tue–Thu, 09:00–12:00 ET.

## 2. Lobste.rs
Tags: `security`, `practices`
Same title. Lobste.rs rewards the measurement framing over the story framing.

## 3. r/programming and r/netsec
r/netsec is the better fit and stricter — lead with the advisory numbers, not
the CVE anecdote.

## 4. Direct, and highest value
The advisory benchmark is built entirely on curl's published data. Daniel
Stenberg has written repeatedly and publicly about AI slop in curl's bug
bounty. Mail him the numbers — not a pitch, just "I measured this against your
206 advisories, here's what I found, thought you'd want to see it."

He is the single highest-value reader of this post and you already did the work
he'd care about.

## 5. Reply where the conversation already is
Search for recent threads on AI-generated bug reports / bounty spam and reply
with the measurement. Do not drop a link cold — answer the question being asked
and mention it did the measuring.

## What to lead with
The CVE anecdote is the hook. The 206-advisory benchmark is the substance. The
admission that no maintainer has run it on real traffic is what makes it
credible rather than a pitch — do not cut it.
